# PR6 phase B — the fenced swap (summary)

Scope: production code only. A barrier-mode GC chain reaching `gc.phase="sweep"`
no longer fences on `refs_version` and aborts — `gc_sweep` drains the write
side's reachability inside the same job (the barrier already feeds
`gc_pending` + gen-1 frontier rows) and then runs the fenced, subset-aware
swap, or the bounded-retry lossless fallback. Flag-off chains, compact chains
and pre-PR6 barrier cohorts (`marked.built IS NULL`) keep the byte-for-byte
PR5 sweep.

## Files changed

- `server/src/jobs/gc.rs` — the swap itself and every helper below.
- `server/src/jobs/compact.rs` — `release_hold` (§3.2 step 11) plus the
  `cohort_live` guard the swap's step 4 shares with `begin_build` and the
  compact swap; `end_of_chain` also calls `compact::trigger`.
- `server/src/jobs/janitor.rs` — hosts the `janitor.before_r2_delete`
  error-mode point used by the phase A crash matrix.
- `server/src/jobs/mod.rs` — generic dispatch: `run_slice` routes `GcSweep`
  to `gc::gc_sweep`; the outcome lander (Done/Continue/Reschedule/retry) is
  what the sweep's multi-slice drain rides on. No swap logic lives here.
- `server/src/repo_do/mod.rs` — `commit_push`'s persisted-barrier read and
  the write-ahead feeds the drain consumes:
  `barrier_write_pending_before_promote` (§3.2: the `gc_pending` row and the
  `refs_version` bump land BEFORE the pack promotion) and
  `barrier_feed_landing` (§3.4: a landing ref feeds new/peeled targets to
  `gc_frontier` at gen 1, minus `gc_seen`, before the ref write).
- `server/src/store/mod.rs` — `schema::LATE_COLS` entry
  `("marked","built","built BLOB")`: the C11 as-copied snapshot column the
  subset check reads; NULL on flag-off/compact chains and pre-PR6 rows
  (the deploy oracle).

## spec step → function (all in `gc.rs` unless noted)

- **§2 deploy rule / sweep entry** — `gc_sweep`: legacy/stray/compact
  guards, `gc.sweep.before`, then `!barrier_active || built_null` →
  `pr5_sweep` (unchanged PR5: `refs_moved` → `abort`, else epoch bump +
  whole-cohort retire). `built_null` is the deploy oracle; `begin_build`
  freezes `built=bitmap` at admission so the C11 check compares what was
  copied. `barrier_active`/`barrier_capture` make the mode a property of
  the persisted chain, never the env var.
- **§3.1 the drain step** — `walk_step(d, …, want_phase=Some("sweep"), …)`:
  the shared mark/drain step — `pending_page` first (§5), then the min-gen
  pop, live-row lookup, bit plan, chunked R2 reads, fenced `commit_ids`.
  `phase_fence` (lease CAS + `gc.chain` equality + phase gate) is the write
  fence every drain span passes; `delta_round` counts gen-1 pops.
- **§3.2 steps 1–3 (the check)** — `swap_check`, one sync span at the top
  of every drain iteration: step 1 `phase_fence` → `gc.swap.before` →
  step 2 dead-pack `gc_pending` prune + the absent-baseline init
  (`swap_meta`) → step 3 the BOTH-empty gate.
- **§3.2 steps 4–6 (quiesced swap)** — `fenced_swap`: step 4
  `compact::cohort_live` (a non-live cohort pack is a corruption wipe, not
  an error) → step 5 `gc.new_pack` must be live → step 6 the C11 subset
  check per cohort row: retirable iff `bm_bitmap_subset(bitmap, built)`
  (`bitmap & !built == 0`), lossy iff `bm_lossy(built, count)` — then
  `retire_span`, or `wipe_path` when the retire set is empty (6a).
- **§3.2 step 7–8 (retirement)** — `retire_span`: the `gc.retire`
  `RetireIntent{ids,lossy,fallback}` upsert is the retirement's first write
  (8a resumes on it); the lossy `gc_epoch` bump precedes every destructive
  write, with `gc.swap.after_epoch` after it. `retire_finish`: the
  one-statement dead-mark of exactly the recorded ids, `swap_stats`
  (kept counters read post-dead-mark), `gc.swap.after_deadmark` or
  `gc.swap.fallback.after_deadmark`, the object-row delete, `end_of_chain`.
- **§3.2 8a resume** — `retire_resume`: `gc.retire` at sweep entry —
  all recorded ids still live → delete the intent and re-validate from
  step 2 (a push since may have resurrected a recorded pack); all dead →
  `retire_finish` for exactly the recorded ids; mixed/corrupt → `wipe_path`.
- **§3.2 5a/6a wipe** — `wipe_path`: dead-pack object rows first, then
  `wipe_all`'s keep rule (cohort intact ⇒ N dead-marked; not intact ⇒ N
  kept live as the surviving copy), then `end_of_chain`. `wipe_all` itself
  is 8b: a `gc.retire` intent interrupted mid-flight still drops its
  already-dead ids' object rows.
- **§3.2 steps 9–11 (end of chain)** — `end_of_chain`: the durable
  `gc_last_swap` record (`retired/kept/retired_bytes/kept_bytes/lossy/
  fallback/tries/slices` from `swap_stats` + `SwapMeta`) and `gc_fallbacks`
  (incremented on fallback, reset on a fenced swap) — both outside `gc.*`
  so they survive the wipe — then the PR5 wipe order, `compact::trigger`
  and `compact::release_hold` (pulls a busy-rescheduled `gc_compact`
  forward to now and pushes every queued `gc_mark` behind
  `now + max(gc_quiet_ms,1)` while a compact row is due).
- **§3.3 tries/slices + fallback** — `SwapMeta{rv,tries,slices}` via
  `swap_meta`/`swap_meta_store` (one upsert, never torn; the baseline
  publishes with phase=sweep and an absent row initialises without
  charging). `swap_check` charges a try when a non-empty check also saw
  `refs_version` move; a `Continue` slice charges one slice under the
  fence. `tries >= swap_tries (8) || slices >= swap_max_slices (64)` →
  `fallback_span`: steps 4–5 again, then the duplicate-aware retirable
  predicate (`bm_all_set` over entries with no live non-cohort copy) and
  the net-positive rule (`ret_bytes >= bytes(N)` — else `wipe_path`),
  then `retire_span(fallback=true, lossy=false)` — NO epoch bump.
